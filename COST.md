# COST — Wood-style review

**Decision: pass for this mandate.**

The shared 10-K series shows revenue rising from 226,954,000,000 at the 2022-08-28 fiscal year end to 303,154,000,000 at 2026-08-30. The scripted first-to-last annual growth is 7.51%, so the classroom 5% revenue-growth check **passes**. The latest `ResearchAndDevelopmentExpense` is **not found**, so the classroom 5% R&D/revenue check is **not known**, not a failure. Net income and the owner-earnings proxy also rise in the selected years. These are encouraging business facts but do not establish an innovation thesis.

My judgment is that this evidence does not make Costco a disruptive-innovation investment. The selected filing fields describe a strong retailer, but do not establish an S-curve, expanding technology market, falling unit costs, or platform convergence. I would need evidence of a specific innovation thesis, adoption, cost curve, and price relative to value. This is a mandate-fit judgment, not a claim that Costco is a poor business or that Cathie Wood would trade it.

**Threshold kept: 5% annual revenue growth for the classroom probe, because it shows that a passing growth screen alone does not establish a disruptive-innovation thesis.**
